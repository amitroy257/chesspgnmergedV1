Everything runs in this page and nothing is uploaded. A chapter merges by name alone: every line with moves and no finished result joins one game, even if some open with a different first move - they just become their own variation before move one. A line with no moves, a genuine custom position, or a finished result (model games, tactics) is copied through instead, in its original place in the chapter. Every move is checked against the board, so a move a course only mentions inside a comment stays text instead of becoming a phantom move. Override any chapter with its own dropdown; your format options are remembered in this browser. Merging matches moves by notation, so lines reaching the same position by a different move order stay on separate branches.
